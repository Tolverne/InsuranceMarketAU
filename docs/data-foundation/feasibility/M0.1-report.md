# M0.1 — Australian vehicle data source feasibility

**Status:** Research findings and executable inspection plan, **not** an ingested or fully validated national fleet.  
**Research date:** 2026-11-21 (Australia/Brisbane).  
**Source focus:** Official Australian government primary sources and a government-data discovery index where primary portal access was restricted.  
**Implementation scope:** Registration stock, attribute enrichment, flows, reference date, licensing and confidentialisation. Risk/claims, household and policy variables are later milestones.

## Executive assessment

**Feasible for M1, subject to obtaining/verifying source payloads.** The main change since the original M0 architecture is discovery of **17 separately published national BITRE January 2026 detailed cross-tabulations plus explanatory notes**. Several pair vehicle type + state + postcode + manufacture year, or type + state + postcode + make/model, or type + make/model + year + motive power. This materially reduces the need to reconstruct every state solely from a few detailed jurisdiction files.

It **does not** mean that an observed national table of \`postcode × make × model × year × fuel\` exists. The available tables overlap in different projections, and have been **confidentialised**. They cannot be merged row-by-row or equated with genuine vehicle-level microdata. Their joint inconsistency, suppression and unknown-category rules must be assessed from the actual downloads and explanatory notes.

**Feasibility verdict:** Proceed with an evidence-first national aggregated population design, then synthesize member-level vehicle rows *conditionally* under documented constraints. The first demonstrator should be an aggregate comparison/query tool, not the 22m+ row population immediately.

## Discovery confidence versus inspection

We use explicit statuses:

- **Publisher-page verified:** Primary government site/source record and known publication/grain/date inspected.
- **Catalogue corroborated:** Another metadata registry describes the primary resource, but original download not independently parsed.
- **Schema preview inspected:** The publisher exposes field names/types on an accessible HTML page.
- **Downloaded and parsed:** Actual bytes retrieved, checksum computed, file/header/sample inspected.
- **Licensing cleared:** A separate data-rights decision authorizes intended storage, commercial use and redistribution.

**No actual bytes were downloaded and parsed in this M0.1 run.** Several binary CSV/XLSX/ZIP links were not fetchable in the available research environment. A portable local inspection script has been added for an analyst to run where those resources are accessible. The statuses below must **not** be upgraded to "downloaded and parsed" based on preview metadata alone.

## Data opportunity hierarchy

1. **BITRE national 2026 confidentialised cross-tabulations**: master independent benchmark matrix and starting synthetic joint distributions; reference **31 January 2026**. [BITRE source](https://www.bitre.gov.au/resource/roads-vehicles/road-vehicles-australia-january-2026) · [catalogue details (ARDC/RDA)](https://researchdata.edu.au/road-vehicles-australia-january-2026/4251449) · [underlying dataset](https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/060ca7bb-83b0-4c9f-a58d-d7d1e7ec6083).
2. **Victorian Q3 2026 stock cross-tab**: garage postcode × make × vehicle class × manufacture year × coarse fuel type × count (publisher preview verifies fields, though not all fields explicitly listed in data dictionary). [Victoria](https://opendata.transport.vic.gov.au/dataset/whole-fleet-vehicle-registration-snapshot-by-postcode). Separate make/model cross-tabs exist but should not be joined one-to-one.
3. **NSW 2026 month-end snapshots and transactions**: annual ZIP resources with **monthly** count data for registered fleet, vehicle age, usage and heavy-vehicle configuration; not confirmed to have a combined postcode × make × model table. [TfNSW](https://data.nsw.gov.au/data/en/dataset/2-transport-nsw-vehicle-registration-statistics).
4. **Queensland historical records / more current controls**: individual-like 10 October 2022 registered extracts and suburb counts, excluding seasonal vehicles; newer registration/transfer flows plus 30 June 2026 registered fleet aggregate reports must be kept separate. [QLD stock](https://www.data.qld.gov.au/dataset/vehicle-registrations) · [QLD flow](https://www.data.qld.gov.au/dataset/vehicle-registration-new-and-transfers-test) · [current controls](https://www.tmr.qld.gov.au/safety/transport-and-road-statistics/registration-statistics).
5. **SA 30 June 2026 joint stocks**: postcode of **owner's residential address** × make × body type × cylinders × manufacture year, historical annual versions back to 2012. Source catalogue states CC BY 4.0. Primary portal blocked during this run; needs payload inspection. [ARDC catalogue](https://researchdata.edu.au/registered-vehicles-postcode/2210007) · [source dataset](https://data.sa.gov.au/data/dataset/7be2f98a-e042-4990-8513-7e176fa1ccc3).
6. **ACT frequently refreshed suppressed joint stocks**: suburb × make × model × emissions category × count, with count **≤10 cells suppressed**. Not a direct complete population total. Includes trailers/motorbikes and records with uncertain spelling/classification. [ACT](https://www.data.act.gov.au/Transport/ACT-Registered-Vehicles-by-Make-Model-and-Suburb/h4vn-y97q).
7. **Tasmania**: current HTML registration stock by **vehicle type**, municipality, premium class and EV body type; beware differing official scope denominators and minor apparent internal discrepancy between tables. [Tasmania](https://www.transport.tas.gov.au/registration/general_information/statistics).
8. **WA**: state fleet can be benchmarked from BITRE, with Q3 2026 **EV licensing** totals by BEV/PHEV plus other breakdowns; a granular full-fleet publicly downloadable stock by make × model × postcode has **not been verified**. The department has its own Western Australian Vehicle Fleet Model, but no public dataset implied. [WA EV data](https://transport.wa.gov.au/projects-planning/electric-vehicles/western-australian-electric-vehicle-registrations) · [WAVFM](https://transport.wa.gov.au/projects-planning/transport-insights/areas-of-enquiry/western-australian-vehicle-fleet-model).
9. **NT**: public historic quarterly registered vehicle-type totals (source page visible to 1 July 2024) and current EV totals (31 October 2026), but **no comprehensive public full fleet joint stock** verified; NT Government vehicle fleet datasets cover only vehicles utilized by agencies and are **not** a proxy for all NT registrations. [NT registration stats](https://roadsafety.nt.gov.au/research-and-statistics/driver-licensing-and-registration-statistics) · [NT EV](https://dli.nt.gov.au/strategies/electric-vehicle) · [NT government fleet](https://data.nt.gov.au/dataset/northern-territory-fleet).

## Jurisdiction feasibility matrix

Grades: **A** strong recent public multi-dimensional joint stock; **B** partial joint/monthly stock or older detailed source; **C** mostly aggregate controls with no confirmed detailed public fleet cross-tab. Grade assesses *published granularity and freshness*, **not** final data quality/permission.

| Region | Best located stock | Detail / geography | Recency and caveat | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Australia | BITRE 2026 cross-tabs | state, registered/garage postcode, type, year, make/model, fuel in **separate projections** | Jan 2026; confidentialised cells; definitions to verify | **A** |
| NSW | TfNSW monthly 2026 ZIP snapshots | monthly counts; age and usage in separate files | March 2026 resource update; actual ZIP layout uninspected | **B** |
| VIC | Transport Victoria Q3 2026 | garage postcode × make × type × year × fuel; separate model | Sep 2026; trailers/boats excluded; coarse “E” category | **A** |
| QLD | TMR 2022 extracts + 2026 reports | historic make/year/fuel/body; suburb counts separate | old stock, latest detailed transactions are **flows** | **B** |
| SA | DIT postcode joint stock | owner postcode × make × body × cylinder × year | Jun 2026 per catalogue, primary files uninspected | **A (provisional)** |
| WA | BITRE state extract + DTMI EV | BITRE national postcode/model partial projections; WA EV segment controls | no new full-fleet state microdata confirmed | **C** |
| TAS | Transport Services live statistics | type, municipality, premium class; no make × model | Oct/Nov 2026; tables may contain trailers and restricted vehicles | **C** |
| ACT | Access Canberra joint count | suburb × make × model × emissions rating | updated Nov 2026; suppression ≤10 cells | **B** |
| NT | Road Safety quarterly / NT EV | vehicle class totals; EV class | historic total/EV current; possible publication lag | **C** |

**Important:** even grade C regions benefit from BITRE’s nationally consistent joint extracts. No region is presumed missing completely.

## Three high-priority risks

### R1 — Multiple BITRE table projections do not form a direct microdata join

Take marginals \`P(make,model,postcode,type)\` and \`P(make,model,year,type)\`: there are multiple joint distributions consistent with both. They do not identify the interaction of postcode and manufacture year within make/model. Fit a constrained conditional model, report evidence support and measure sensitivity; do **not** duplicate vehicle counts by joining category totals.

### R2 — Confidentialisation and suppressed cells

BITRE says extracts are confidentialised; ACT explicitly suppresses aggregate counts **≤10**. Confidentialised values may be perturbed or censored rather than exact. Extract original notes and code sentinels and define interval constraints where appropriate. Structural zero, observed zero, hidden/suppressed count and unavailable field are separate. Hard-constraining every cross-tab could render the optimization infeasible. Preserve aggregate and suppressed-cell provenance and small-cell safeguards.

### R3 — Geography and vintage are not comparable by default

Victoria reports garaging postcode; South Australia describes owner residential postcode; some BITRE files distinguish registered from garaging postcode; NSW states monthly counts and Tasmania gives municipality. Keep \`geography_role\` plus \`geography_system\` and \`vintage\` explicit. Refreshed jurisdictional 2026 sources are a **validation/calibration at their own observation dates**, not automatically hard controls for a January 2026 national base.

## Proposed M1 strategy

### Phase A: national constraints first

Fetch and profile the BITRE XLSX and *all* 2026 detailed CSV files and notes. Catalogue each cross-tab's row grain, suppression mechanism, unit, geography type, unknowns, total and publication/update hashes. Establish a national state/class benchmark and compare every aggregate projection under documented tolerances.

### Phase B: independent jurisdiction evidence

Read VIC and SA detailed cross-tabs and NSW ZIPs; use 2026 QLD totals as side controls and 2022 microdata as conditional reference. Record ACT suppression and TAS/WA/NT segment controls. Detect date and scope discrepancies, **not** simply compare unadjusted totals.

### Phase C: synthesis baseline

Start with a multi-way sparse contingency base keyed by vehicle type, state, location role/postcode, make/model, manufacture year and fuel with attached observed constraints and structural-zero rules. Use a prior derived from stronger joint distributions, then constrained fitting with uncertainty bands. Publish aggregate synthetic diagnostics and weighted-cohort samples before full vehicle instance materialization. Validate withheld projections and compare multiple plausible joint structures.

### Phase D: vehicle-level export

Only after aggregate reconciliation and benchmark reviews, produce vehicle-level synthetic rows and optional weighted sample. Synthetic records must not be presented as actual registration records. M2 adds households, drivers, insurance cover, usage and insured values.

### Out-of-scope-but-needed data assets

Vehicle value, trim/variant/specifications, risk geography, household/driver relationships, annual km, insurance penetration, quote behaviour and claims are not supplied by these registration sources in the detail our simulator needs. They remain separate source-enrichment/backlog streams; do not synthesize insurance demand into M1 fleet data.
