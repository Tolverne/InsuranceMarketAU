# ADR-0002 — Symmetric participants in one competitive market

- **Status:** Proposed (M0)
- **Context:** A client, startup or competitor must not use special acquisition/retention logic. A group may own multiple competing brands.
- **Decision:** Model group → risk carrier/agent → brand → product/offer explicitly. All offers use the same target-market, underwriting, pricing and choice pipeline; a target company is simply a reporting perspective.
- **Consequences:** Group-level market share, cannibalisation, legal-risk placement and financial consolidation become possible. Need configurable offer visibility and conflicts; avoid treating brand as synonymous with risk carrier.
- **Review when:** Legal/risk arrangements demand additional underwriting capacity or delegated authority dimensions.
